Knock recordings: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain).
Source: "Chess_foley.mp3" by Amatsuuu on Freesound, "chess pieces hitting the
wood table and board", through Chess's move-1.mp3 to move-4.mp3 in this
repository (see public/chess/sounds/LICENSE.txt). Two knocks mixed about
0.13 s apart, as knuckles rap twice on a table, low-passed at 1.6-1.7 kHz,
faded, trimmed to 0.4 s and converted to mono MP3.
https://freesound.org/people/Amatsuuu/sounds/629181/
