How to play
  • Two players share a grid. One plays X, the other plays O, and X always moves first.
  • Before a game, pick the board: 3 × 3, where three in a row wins, or 5 × 5, where you need four in a row.
  • Pick who moves first: a coin toss drawn by both browsers, you, or your opponent. When you play a friend, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to both of you.
  • Take turns marking one empty square. Line up your marks across, down or corner to corner to win. A full board with no line is a draw.
  • Clocks (optional): a limit for each move, and a total for each player's whole game. Whoever runs out of time loses the round.
  • Press Rematch for another round with the same settings. The score counts your wins, draws and losses while you both stay.
  • On a keyboard, move between squares with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to play.
  • Fair play: both browsers check every move with the same rules, so a move that breaks them stops the match. Each browser keeps its own player's clock.
