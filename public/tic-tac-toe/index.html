How to play
  • Two players share a 3×3 grid. One plays X, the other plays O.
  • A coin toss drawn by both browsers decides who starts. Whoever starts plays X.
  • Take turns marking one empty square. A marked square stays put for the rest of the round.
  • Line up three of your marks across, down or corner to corner to win.
  • If all nine squares fill up with no line of three, the round is a draw.
  • Press Rematch for another round with a fresh coin toss. The score counts your wins, draws and losses while you both stay.
  • On a keyboard, move between squares with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to play.
  • Fair play: both browsers check every move with the same rules, so a move that breaks them stops the match.
