How to play
  • Each player hides a fleet of 5 ships (sizes 5, 4, 3, 3 and 2) on a 10×10 grid. Ships can't touch side by side; corners are fine.
  • Ships are placed at random. Shuffle, drag ships to move them, or tap a ship to rotate it, then press Ready.
  • Take turns firing at the enemy waters. A hit lets you fire again; a miss passes the turn. Fire marks a hit, a splash marks a miss.
  • When a ship is fully hit it sinks and is revealed. The water beside it is marked as clear.
  • Every few moves a gift pops up on each board. Hit it to win a weapon: Simple missile (a bonus shot, your turn continues), Big missile (5-square plus), Missile rain (7 random squares) or Nuclear missile (14-square blast). A gift left on water cleared beside a sunk ship disappears.
  • Sink the whole enemy fleet to win.
  • Fair play: your fleet never leaves your browser until the game ends. Both sides lock in their fleet with a cryptographic commitment and check each other's answers afterwards. Every random event (who starts, gifts, missile rain) is drawn jointly by both browsers.
