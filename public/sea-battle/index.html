How to play
  • Each player hides a fleet of 5 ships (sizes 5, 4, 3, 3 and 2) on a 10×10 grid. Ships can't touch side by side; corners are fine.
  • Ships are placed at random. Shuffle, or drag ships to move them. While dragging, press R to rotate the ship; you can also tap a ship to rotate it, or use the arrow keys and R on a focused ship. Then press Ready.
  • Take turns firing at the enemy waters. A hit lets you fire again; a miss, or sinking a ship, passes the turn. Fire marks a hit, a splash marks a miss.
  • When a ship is fully hit it sinks and is revealed. The water beside it is marked as clear.
  • Before you start, press Change under your nickname to pick the time limits: time per shot (if it runs out, a random shot is fired for you) and time for each player (if your clock runs out, you lose). With a friend, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to both of you; against the robot, only your clock runs.
  • Every few moves a mystery gift (?) pops up in the enemy waters (your opponent gets one in yours, which you don't see). Aim at its own square to win a weapon (a blast or rain that hits it from another square destroys it): Big missile (5-square plus), Missile rain (7 random squares; select it, then press Launch rain), Nuclear missile (14-square blast) or Carpet bomb (a whole row or column; press R or the Row/Column button to switch). A gift left on water cleared beside a sunk ship disappears.
  • Sink the whole enemy fleet to win.
  • Fair play: your fleet never leaves your browser until the game ends. Both sides lock in their fleet with a cryptographic commitment and check each other's answers afterwards. Every random event (who starts, gifts, missile rain) is drawn jointly by both browsers.
