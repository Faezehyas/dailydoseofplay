How to play
  • The board stands upright. Pick a column and your disc slides down to the lowest empty spot in it.
  • Take turns dropping one disc each. The first to line up four of their colour across, up and down, or on a slant wins. If every column fills up first, it's a draw.
  • Coral always moves first. Pick who that is: a coin toss drawn by both browsers, you, or your opponent.
  • Pick the board: the classic 7 × 6, or a roomier 8 × 7, 8 × 8, 9 × 7 or 9 × 9. It's always four in a row.
  • Clocks (optional): a limit for each move, and a total for each player's whole game. Whoever runs out of time loses the round.
  • Playing the robot? Choose Easy, Medium or Hard. When you play a friend, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to both of you.
  • Press Rematch for another round with the same settings. The score counts your wins, draws and losses while you both stay.
  • On a keyboard, move between columns with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to drop.
  • Fair play: both browsers check every move with the same rules, so a move that breaks them stops the match. Each browser keeps its own player's clock.
