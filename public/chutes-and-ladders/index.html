How to play
  • Two to four players each have a pawn, and all start on the lawn below square 1. Take turns spinning the spinner and hop forward that many squares, along the rows: left to right, up a row, right to left, and so on up to 100 at the top left.
  • Stop at the foot of a ladder and you climb straight up to its top. Stop at the top of a chute and you slide all the way down it. Only the square you stop on counts, not the ones you hop over.
  • Pawns can share a square; nobody gets bumped.
  • The first to reach square 100 wins. In the classic game you need the exact number: if your spin would take you past 100, you stay where you are.
  • Before a game you can change that: Bounce back walks you back from 100 by the steps left over, and Any spin lets any spin that reaches 100 win. You can also let a 6 earn another spin, and pick who spins first. When you play friends, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to everyone.
  • To play friends, create a room and send the invite link to up to three of them. Press Start game when everyone is in; a full room of four starts by itself. Against the robot, pick 1, 2 or 3 robots in the settings.
  • Press Spin or tap the spinner when it's your turn. On a keyboard, Tab to the Spin button and press Enter or Space. Pick Spins by itself in the settings to sit back and watch.
  • Fair play: every spin is drawn by all the players' browsers together, so nobody can choose or predict it, and every browser checks every move with the same rules.
