How to play
  • Both pawns start on the lawn below square 1. Take turns spinning the spinner and hop forward that many squares, along the rows: left to right, up a row, right to left, and so on up to 100 at the top left.
  • Stop at the foot of a ladder and you climb straight up to its top. Stop at the top of a chute and you slide all the way down it. Only the square you stop on counts, not the ones you hop over.
  • Both pawns can share a square; nobody gets bumped.
  • The first to reach square 100 wins. In the classic game you need the exact number: if your spin would take you past 100, you stay where you are.
  • Before a game you can change that: Bounce back walks you back from 100 by the steps left over, and Any spin lets any spin that reaches 100 win. You can also let a 6 earn another spin, and pick who spins first. When you play a friend, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to both of you.
  • Press Spin or tap the spinner when it's your turn. On a keyboard, Tab to the Spin button and press Enter or Space. Pick Spins by itself in the settings to sit back and watch.
  • Fair play: every spin is drawn by both browsers together, so neither can choose or predict it, and both check every move with the same rules.
