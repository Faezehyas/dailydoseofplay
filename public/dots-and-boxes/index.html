How to play
  • The board starts as a grid of dots. Take turns drawing one line between two dots that sit side by side, across or down. Diagonals don't count.
  • Draw the fourth side of a box and it's yours: it fills with your colour and your initial. Closing a box (or two at once) means you draw again straight away, so one turn can sweep up a whole row.
  • When every box is taken, whoever has the most wins. An even split is a draw.
  • The trap: drawing a box's third side hands it to your opponent, and often the boxes behind it too. Draw safe lines for as long as you can.
  • Near the end, when every line gives something away, it can pay to leave the last two boxes of a long chain: your opponent takes them but then has to open the next chain for you.
  • Before a game you can pick the board (3×3 up to 6×6 boxes), a time per line and per player, and who draws first. When you play a friend, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to both of you.
  • Tap or click a gap between two dots to draw there; on a computer, hovering shows the line first. On a keyboard, Tab to the board, move with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to draw.
  • Fair play: both browsers check every line with the same rules, and the coin toss for who starts is drawn by both together.
