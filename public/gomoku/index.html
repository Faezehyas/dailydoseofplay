How to play
  • Two players take turns placing one stone on an empty point of a 15 × 15 grid. Stones never move and are never taken.
  • The first player's stones are solid; the second player's have a ring. The first stone can go anywhere.
  • Get five of your stones in an unbroken line across, down or diagonally to win. Six or more in a row counts too.
  • If every point fills up and nobody has five, the round is a draw.
  • Pick who moves first: a coin toss drawn by both browsers, you, or your opponent. Moving first is a small edge, so take turns over a few rounds.
  • Clocks (optional): a limit for each move, and a total for each player's whole game. Whoever runs out of time loses the round. When you play a friend, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to both of you.
  • Tip: an open three (three in a row with both ends free) must be blocked at once, or it becomes an open four that can't be stopped.
  • Press Rematch for another round with the same settings. The score counts your wins, draws and losses while you both stay.
  • On a keyboard, Tab to the board, move between points with the arrow keys, and press Enter or Space to play.
  • Fair play: both browsers check every move with the same rules, so a move that breaks them stops the match. Each browser keeps its own player's clock.
