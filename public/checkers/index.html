How to play
  • Each player starts with 12 pieces on the dark squares of an 8 × 8 board, in the three rows nearest to them. Pieces only ever stand on dark squares.
  • Pick who moves first: a coin toss drawn by both browsers, you, or your opponent. When you play a friend, the settings of whoever creates the room apply to both of you.
  • On your turn, move one piece diagonally forward to a free square next to it.
  • Capture by hopping over an opponent's piece next to yours onto the free square just behind it. If you can capture, you must. If, after a hop, the same piece can hop again, it must keep going in the same turn. When several captures are possible, you choose which.
  • A piece that reaches the far row becomes a king (it gets a crown), and its turn ends there. Kings move and capture diagonally backwards as well as forwards, one square at a time.
  • You win when your opponent has no pieces left, or none that can move. After 40 turns in a row with no capture and no new king, the game is a draw.
  • Clocks (optional): a limit for each move, and a total for each player's whole game. Whoever runs out of time loses the round.
  • To move, tap one of your pieces, then the square to land on. For a multi-hop, tap each landing square in turn, or just the last one. Pieces that can move are marked when it's your turn.
  • On a keyboard, move between dark squares with the arrow keys, press Enter or Space to pick a piece or a square, and Escape to put the piece back.
  • Against the robot, pick Easy, Medium or Hard before you start.
  • Sounds: a wooden clack when a piece lands, a knock for each piece captured, and a chime for a new king. The speaker button at the top mutes them on this device.
  • Press Rematch for another round with the same settings. The score counts your wins, draws and losses while you both stay.
  • Fair play: both browsers check every move with the same rules, so a move that breaks them stops the match. Each browser keeps its own player's clock.
